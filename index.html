FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is BackgroundBlaster free?

Yes. The site uses a shared monthly quota for AI background removal. If it runs out, you can add your own free remove.bg API key under and keep going.

Which formats can I download?

PNG (keeps transparency), JPG, WEBP and BMP — plus AVIF in browsers that support it. You can also download a 4 × 6 in print sheet with several copies of your photo.

Will my passport photo be accepted?

We size photos to the official dimensions at 300 DPI and position the head automatically, but every country has its own rules about background colour, expression, glasses and lighting. Always check the official requirements before submitting.

What happens to my photos?

Your photo is sent securely to remove.bg only to cut out the background. All editing, resizing and exporting happens locally in your browser — nothing is stored on our side.

How do I get the best result?

Use a well-lit, sharp photo where the subject is clearly separated from the background. For passport photos, face the camera directly with your shoulders visible.
